Bar 1 — Black Blood of Y'Shaarj proc

"Wrath of the Darkspear" (146184) lasts 10 s, ticking every second; each tick adds a stack of "Wrath" (146202, the real Intellect buff, 2 s duration refreshed by every tick). Stacks reach 10 at second 10 and linger ~2 s after the bar ends — that tail is the gold snapshot window. Text: stacks (left), seconds remaining (right). Style: 300×30, "Solid" texture, green, white ticks at 1–9 s, trinket icon on the left. Works for every item version.

Bar 2 — Sinister Primal Diamond proc

Meta gem "Sinister Primal Diamond" procs "Tempus Repit" (137590): flat +30% spell haste for 10 s, no stacks. 9 tick marks at 1–9 s. The bar drains for the full 10 s and disappears the moment the buff falls off — no tail. Style: 300×30 "Solid" bar, blue, meta gem's own icon on the left.

Bar 3 — Unerring Vision of Lei Shen proc

Trinket "Unerring Vision of Lei Shen" procs "Perfect Aim" (138963): flat +100% critical strike chance for 4 s, no stacks. 3 tick marks at 1–3 s. Very short window — the bar drains fast and disappears the instant it falls off. Works for every item version (LFR/Celestial, Normal, Thunderforged, Heroic, Heroic Thunderforged — all share the same spell IDs). Style: 300×30 "Solid" bar, orange/red, trinket's own icon, centered time text (matches this trinket's look in your original base aura).
Position numbers on all three blocks are in WeakAuras units, anchor CENTER → CENTER. Positive Y = up. All three bars live in the same parent dynamic group: only the procs that are currently active are shown, auto-stacked with no gap for an inactive one — nothing to reconfigure if you don't have all 3 trinkets. In-game, dragging the group moves all of them together; here each bar is draggable independently for previewing layout. Screen is 1366×768 units (1 unit ≈ 1.41 px at 1080p, ≈ 1.88 px at 1440p).
