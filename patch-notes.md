## v2.0.0 — Initial release

Three proc trackers in one dynamic group:

- **Black Blood of Y'Shaarj** — Wrath of the Darkspear timeline with per-second ticks; turns gold at 9+ stacks (snapshot window); plays Error Beep.
- **Sinister Primal Diamond** — Tempus Repit (+30% haste) tracker with tick marks; plays Electrical Spark.
- **Unerring Vision of Lei Shen** — Perfect Aim (+100% crit) tracker with tick marks; plays Boxing Arena Gong.

Bars auto-stack with no gaps for whichever procs aren't currently active — nothing to configure per trinket.
